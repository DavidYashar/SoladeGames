3.Game 1 — Daily Puzzle League 🧩

Gameplay

  • One puzzle per day, same for everyone. Resets at midnight UTC.
  • Mixed puzzle types, rotating daily LOCKED — mixture keeps it fresh, never boring:
    • 🔤 Word days — Wordle-style word guessing
    • 🔢 Number days — math / number-sequence riddles
    • 🧠 Logic days — pattern / logic grid puzzles
  • Free mode: everyone plays free → score + shareable score card (Wordle-style virality).
  • Streaks & XP: solve streaks → XP → free entries, badges, entry discounts.
  • Weekly Championship and referral bonuses (5% of a friend's first entry, paid from our 15%).

How users make money

  1. Winning leagues — the direct prize, paid instantly in USDC
  2. Streaks → free entries — consistency converts into free shots at prizes
  3. Referrals — small % of friends' entries
  4. Sponsor quests — brands sponsor puzzle days; quests pay micro-USDC

The NFT — League Seat COLLECTION 1

  • Fixed supply: 10,000 seats. Ever.
  • Each seat = 1/10,000 of the daily Holder pool (25% of entries + 50% of cosmetics).
  • Tiers: 🥉 Bronze (standard share) · 🥈 Silver (2× share + 1 free entry/day) · 🥇 Gold (5× share + free gold entry + vote on puzzle themes).
  • Upgrading burns lower seats → supply shrinks over time → scarcity grows.
  • 5% royalty on every resale → to us.

4.Game 2 — Arcade King 🕹️

Gameplay

  • Flagship game: one polished endless-runner / stacker with a daily challenge mode.
  • Later: 2–3 mini-games (marble drop, memory, aim trainer).
  • Free mode: unlimited play, leaderboards, daily challenge, score-clip sharing.
  • Paid runs: 0.01–0.10 USDC per run — beat the daily top score to win.
  • Daily Tournament: bracket entry (0.25 USDC), top scores split the pot. Weekend Grand Prix with a bigger pool.
  • Skill-matching (Skillz-style): players in skill bands → everyone feels they can win.

How users make money

  1. Paid runs & tournaments — skill → cash, settled instantly
  2. Daily leaderboard prizes — top free scores get small USDC from a sponsor pool
  3. Streak bonuses — 7 days straight → bonus entry credit
  4. Holding a Machine NFT — passive rake share

The NFT — Arcade Machine COLLECTION 2

  • Fixed supply: 5,000 machines. Each machine = 1/5,000 of the Holder pool.
  • Machine level: games played on a machine's "lobby" earn it XP → level up → owner perks (free daily entries, leaderboard nameplate). Leveling = visible growth of the asset.
  • Rare variants: limited skins (e.g., 1/50 gold-plated, 3× share) as tournament prizes.
  • Own-the-casino narrative: "I own machine #0421 — it pays me every day because people are playing."

7.Fairness & Anti-Abuse

  • Server-side score verification; anti-bot captchas on paid entries
  • One entry per wallet per league; skill-band matching
  • Free tier always available; payouts only from entry fees (never our treasury)
  • Prize pools onchain and auditable
